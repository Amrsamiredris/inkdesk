Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Inkdesk edits PDFs inside your browser. Your documents are never uploaded. The only personal information Inkdesk collects is the name and email you type into the welcome form, so Amr can see which friends use it.

Who is responsible

Inkdesk is a personal project run by Amr Samir Edris, based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. For any privacy question or request, email amrsamiredris@gmail.com.

Your PDFs

Every tool works on your device. Opening, editing, signing, redacting, merging and downloading all happen in your browser’s memory. Your files and their contents are never sent to Amr, to Inkdesk’s hosting provider or to anyone else.

The one exception is the Translate tile. It opens Google Translate in a new tab. If you upload a file there, Google receives it under Google’s Privacy Policy.

What we collect, and why

InformationWhy
Your name and emailSo Amr knows which friends use Inkdesk.
Date of your first and latest visit, and how many times you’ve visitedTo see whether people come back.
Browser and device type (for example “Safari on iOS (phone)”)To know which devices need testing. The full browser identifier is not collected.
The website that linked you here (domain only, for example “linkedin.com”)To know where visitors come from.

Nothing else is collected. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no profiling. Your information is never sold or used for marketing.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent, which you give by submitting the welcome form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing the address above. Withdrawing doesn’t affect anything done before you withdrew.

Where it is stored

Your details are written to a private Google Sheet in Amr’s Google account, through a small Google Apps Script. Google stores this data on its servers, which may be outside the United Arab Emirates. Google acts as a service provider under its own security and privacy terms. Only Amr can open the sheet. When you first sign up, Amr receives an email notification with your name and email.

Inkdesk is hosted by Vercel and GitHub Pages. Like any web host, they process technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure, under their own privacy policies. Inkdesk doesn’t receive or use those logs.

How long it is kept

Until you ask for it to be deleted, or until Inkdesk is shut down, whichever comes first. Amr reviews the list at least once a year and removes entries that are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information held about you, correct it, delete it, or stop its use. Email amrsamiredris@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Requests are handled within 30 days, and usually much sooner. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the UAE Data Office or, if you live in the European Union or the UK, to your local data protection authority.

Storage on your device

Inkdesk uses no cookies. It keeps a few small items in your browser’s local storage so the features you use keep working. These never leave your device:

ItemPurpose
inkdesk.visitorRemembers that you’ve already filled in the welcome form, so you aren’t asked again.
inkdesk.sigsSignatures you chose to save for next time.
inkdesk.authorThe name shown on your comments.
inkdesk.dloptsYour last download settings.
inkdesk.pinged (session only)Counts one visit per browser session.

These are needed for features you ask for, so no cookie banner is shown. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with strict security headers. All code and fonts are loaded from Inkdesk’s own address rather than third-party servers. Access to the visitor sheet is protected by Amr’s Google account. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your information, you’ll be told by email.

Children

Inkdesk is not meant for children under 16. If a child has signed up, email us and the entry will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Significant changes will be shown on the site before they take effect.
